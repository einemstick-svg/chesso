Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short

Chesso does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (lessons, puzzles, streak, badges, settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your phone and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

Chesso Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your payment details, name or email. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account owns Chesso Pro.

Notifications

If you allow reminders, they are scheduled on your phone by iOS. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

Chesso is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

Chesso contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. The chess rules library (chess.js, BSD licence) runs entirely on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
