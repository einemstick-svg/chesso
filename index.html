Common questions

How do I restore Chesso Pro on a new phone?

Open Chesso, tap PRO on the home screen, then "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Chesso Pro is a one-time purchase. You pay once and keep it.

Does Chesso need the internet?

No. Lessons, puzzles, bots and analysis all work offline.

How do I turn off the evening reminder?

iPhone Settings → Notifications → Chesso. You can also turn it off there and back on any time.

How do I change the language or explanation level?

Tap the gear icon on the home screen.

I lost my progress.

Progress is kept only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it too; Pro can always be restored.
